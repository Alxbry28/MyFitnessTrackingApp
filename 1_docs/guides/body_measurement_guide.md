# Body Measurement Guide

**For:** measuring at home from now on, before the app has the feature  
**Matches:** [plan/10 §5](../plan/10_body_progress.md#5-body-measurements-muscle-sizes) and the [measure-guide mockup](../../prototypes/measure-guide.html)  
**Print tip:** open in VS Code's Markdown preview (`Ctrl+Shift+V`) → print, or paste into Google Docs.

---

## 1. What you need

- [ ] **Soft tape measure** (tailor's tape, cm side). Not a metal tape.
- [ ] A **mirror**, to check the tape is level
- [ ] Your phone's notes app, or this page printed

## 2. When

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Morning, after the bathroom, before eating or training** | Food, water and a pump after training all add size |
| **Every 2–4 weeks**, same weekday | Muscle grows slowly; weekly numbers are mostly noise |
| **Weigh yourself first** (Xiaomi scale) | So weight and measurements share a date |

## 3. Golden rules

1. **Same time, same spot, same tape** every session.
2. Tape **snug on the skin and level** all the way around. Don't press in.
3. **Measure twice.** If the two differ, write down the average.
4. Arms: choose **flexed** or **relaxed** once, and never switch. (Plan default: **flexed**.)
5. Stand tall and relaxed, feet hip-width apart. Don't suck in or puff up.

---

## 4. Where and how to measure

**Track these 6 first** (★). Add the others later if you want.

| # | Site | How | Watch out for |
|---|------|-----|---------------|
| ★ | **Chest** | Across the nipples, arms down at your sides. Read after a normal breath out | Tape dropping lower at the back. Check in the mirror |
| ★ | **Waist** | At the navel, standing relaxed. Breathe out normally, then read | Sucking in; measuring at the narrowest point one time and the navel the next |
| ★ | **Upper arm, right** | Raise the arm to shoulder height, **flex** (fist toward shoulder). Tape around the biceps peak, the widest part | Tape slanted toward the elbow |
| ★ | **Upper arm, left** | Same as right | Measuring only your stronger arm |
| ★ | **Thigh, right** | Standing, weight on both legs. Widest part, usually just below the buttocks | Tensing the leg one time and not the next |
| ★ | **Thigh, left** | Same as right | — |
| | Neck | Just below the Adam's apple, looking straight ahead | Tilting the head |
| | Shoulders | Widest point around both shoulders, arms relaxed (easier with help) | Hard to do alone; skip if no one can help |
| | Forearm | Widest point, arm straight, fist relaxed | — |
| | Hips | Widest part of the buttocks, feet together | — |
| | Calf | Widest point, standing, weight on both feet | Standing on tiptoe |

### Mark your spots once (consistency)

Measure the distance from a landmark to where the tape sits, write it here, and reuse it every session:

| Site | Landmark | My distance |
|------|----------|-------------|
| Upper arm | Above the elbow crease | ______ cm |
| Thigh | Above the top of the kneecap | ______ cm |
| Calf (if tracked) | Below the back of the knee | ______ cm |

## 5. Write it down like this

Use the same format every time so the numbers are easy to enter in the app later:

```
2026-10-20 07:00 flexed
weight 69.4
chest 98.5
waist 81.1
arm R 34.5
arm L 34.0
thigh R 56.5
thigh L 56.0
note: slept badly, felt bloated
```

- Date `YYYY-MM-DD` first, then `flexed` or `relaxed`
- One site per line: name, then **cm** (one decimal)
- Skipped a site? Leave the line out. Don't write 0.

## 7. Reading the results

| You see | It probably means |
|---------|-------------------|
| Waist ↓, arms/chest ↑ or same | Losing fat, keeping or building muscle 👍 |
| Waist ↓, arms/chest ↓, weight dropping fast | Losing some muscle too; check protein and calories |
| Waist ↑, arms ↑ | Bulking: some fat comes with muscle; watch the waist |
| Changes under ~0.5 cm | Usually measuring noise; look at 2–3 sessions, not one |
| Left and right differ by ~0.5–1 cm | Normal. A bigger gap that keeps growing is worth noticing |

> These are rules of thumb for tracking trends, not medical advice.
