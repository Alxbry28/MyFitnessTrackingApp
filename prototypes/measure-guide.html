Before you start

Soft tape measure (tailor's tape), not a metal one

Morning, after the bathroom, before eating or training

Light clothing or bare skin; stand in front of a mirror

Relaxed and standing tall, feet hip-width apart

Golden rules

Same time, same spot, same tape every session.

Tape snug on the skin and level all the way around. Don't press in.

Measure twice. If they differ, the app saves the average.

Every 2–4 weeks is enough. Weekly changes are mostly noise.

Pick flexed or relaxed arms once, and keep it.

2 · Site detail: upper arm, with your consistency spot

Upper arm

flexed · your setting

How

Raise your arm to shoulder height and flex, fist toward your shoulder.

Find the biceps peak: the widest part of the upper arm.

Wrap the tape around it, level, crossing the back of the arm (triceps).

Read in cm. Right arm first, then left.

Common mistakes

Tape slanted toward the elbow

Flexed this time, relaxed last time

Pulling the tape tight so it digs in

Same spot every time: measure the distance from your elbow crease to the tape once. The app saves it ("12 cm above the elbow crease") and shows it each session.
