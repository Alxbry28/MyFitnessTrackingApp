Frame 1: Upper Body Guide (Barbell Bench Press)

Chest · Pectorals Barbell Compound
Barbell Bench Press
Target: Pectoralis Major
Assist: Triceps · Ant. Deltoid
🏆
Your Personal Record
82.5 kg × 6 reps
Est. 1RM 96 kg
Execution Checklist
1
Set the Arch & Retract Scapula
Pinch your shoulder blades down and back into the bench. Eyes positioned directly under the bar.
2
Unrack & Establish Wrist Stack
Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder width. Squeeze the bar tightly so wrists don't bend back.
3
Controlled Descent (45°–70° Tuck)
Lower the bar in a diagonal arc to touch the lower sternum. Do not bounce off the ribcage.
4
Leg Drive & Explosive Press
Drive heels through the floor while keeping glutes planted on the bench. Press up and back to lockout.
Critical Form Cues
✓
Tuck elbows at 45°: Protects rotator cuffs from dangerous 90° impingement.
✓
Keep glutes on the bench: Arching upper spine is safe; lifting buttocks loses stability.
✕
Bouncing off chest: Increases sternum injury risk and robs muscle tension.
✕
Bent back wrists: Leads to wrist hyperextension and power leakage.
🫁
Breathing & Valsalva Cue
Take a deep belly breath at the top and brace your core tightly. Hold your breath through the descent, then exhale forcefully past the sticking point on the press.

Frame 2: Lower Body Guide (Barbell Back Squat)

18:46●●● 82%
‹ Exercises Exercise Guide + Add
Legs · Quads & Glutes Barbell King of Compounds
Barbell Back Squat
Target: Quadriceps · Glutes
Assist: Hamstrings · Adductors · Core
🏆
Your Personal Record
105 kg × 5 reps
Est. 1RM 122 kg
Setup & Mechanics
1
Stance Width & Toe Flare
Feet shoulder-width apart, toes turned outward 15°–30° to open hip capsule for proper depth.
2
Bar Placement & Upper Back Shelf
Rest bar tightly on upper trapezius (high bar) or across rear deltoids (low bar). Pull elbows down.
3
Knee & Hip Break Together
Break at hips and knees simultaneously. Push knees out in line with your toes.
4
Hit Depth & Drive from Mid-Foot
Descend until hip crease is below the top of the patella. Drive hips upward evenly.
Common Faults to Avoid
✕
Knee Valgus (Knees caving inward): Puts severe stress on ACL; actively cue "spread the floor".
✕
Good Morning Squat: Hips shooting up before chest causes lower back shear. Keep torso angle rigid.
✓
Tripod Foot Pressure: Distribute weight evenly across heel, big toe knuckle, and pinky toe knuckle.
🛡️
Safety Pin Height
Set rack safety pins 1–2 inches below your bottom parallel depth so you can dump the bar safely without crashing your neck if fatigue strikes.
